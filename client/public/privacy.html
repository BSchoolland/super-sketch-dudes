Super Sketch Dudes: privacy

Super Sketch Dudes is a small hobby game run by Benjamin Schoolland. This is everything it keeps about you.

When you sign in

With Discord or Google, the game asks for your basic profile only and keeps your account ID, display name and avatar picture. It never sees your password, email, contacts, or anything else in those accounts.

With an email and password, it keeps your email, your chosen name, and a salted scrypt hash of your password (never the password itself).

While you play

Characters you draw, what the game generates from them, and match and error logs used to fix bugs and balance fighters. Other players see your name, avatar and fighters.

What it doesn't do

No ads, no tracking across other sites, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Drawings are sent to an AI model to turn them into fighters; nothing else about you is.

Deleting your data

Email bschoolland@gmail.com and your account and everything tied to it will be deleted.
