Terms

Super Sketch Dudes is a free game made by one person ("the developer"). By playing it you agree to these few rules.

Who can have an account

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Making an email account asks your birth year, which isn't stored. Anyone can play as a guest without an account.

What you draw

Your drawings stay yours. By making a character you let the game use the drawing to build a fighter and show it in the game, including to other players if you share it.

Don't draw or name anything rude, hateful, sexual or gory, and don't use other people's real names or pictures. An automatic moderator checks every drawing; the developer can hold back or remove any character, and can stop an account that keeps breaking the rules.

Playing with others

Be decent to other players. Don't cheat, attack the game's servers or try to get into other people's accounts.

The game itself

It's a hobby project, given as it is. It may change, break, lose things or shut down, and characters are made by AI so they can come out strange. Keep anything you care about.

Changes and contact

These terms may change; the game will link the current ones. Questions: bschoolland@gmail.com. How your data is handled is on the privacy page.
